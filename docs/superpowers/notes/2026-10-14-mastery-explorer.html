Mastery model explorer

Two numbers per (student, skill): strength — can they do it? — and stability — will they still have it? The maths from the Slice 1 plan, made playable.

Forgetting curve R(t) = 2^(−(t − last success)/S)

live decay past segments successful review failure due point

The curve drops by half every S days. A review resets it to 1. A failure doesn't reset it — the curve steepens from the same anchor, which is why a lapse feels like losing progress.

Stability history after each review (log scale)

On-time successes at r_target = 0.90 multiply S by ×1.5 each. Fourteen of them carry a skill from "gone by tomorrow" to monthly upkeep. A failure drops it to ×0.3 — about three reviews' worth.

Expected success p = 1 / (1 + e^(−(strength − difficulty)))

            
            

              sigmoid
              current gap
              surprise on correct
              surprise on wrong
            

            

              The gap strength − difficulty is in logits: +1 means p ≈ 0.73, +1.5 means p ≈ 0.82 (the mastery threshold). Attempts move strength, not difficulty — the model moves only by surprise, and only by as much as the weight allows.
            

          

          

            
Strength over attempts

            
            
Each correct answer climbs by K·w·(1−p); hard items and early attempts move more. K = 0.4/(1+0.05·n) shrinks as evidence accumulates.
