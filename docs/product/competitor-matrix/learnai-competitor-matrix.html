How to read the overlap

Continuity is already a competitive category. StudyFetch describes syllabus-driven study plans; CENTURY and IXL describe ongoing personalised pathways. Canvas and Blackboard connect learning to institutional courses and are adding AI. Check scope rather than assuming these are one-off chats.

Unknown is not a missing feature. Public pages rarely disclose exact help-weighted mastery rules, verifier architecture or scheduler parameters. Treat those cells as product-demo questions, not evidence of exclusivity.

Any subject is a coverage strategy. Upload-based breadth, an LMS hosting a history course, and reliable assessment of historical argument are different capabilities. LearnAI's intent includes each subject's content, pedagogy and assessment.
